My process of making this website has been quite an adventure. It was definitely a learning curve,
but also an opportunity to gain a lot of first experiences...

<!-- excerpt -->

### DIY? No, thanks ✋🫩

I cannot quite recall the last time I made a website without drowning in a spaghetti ocean of HTML,
CSS and JavaScript. Fighting to stay afloat, I'd wonder how the hell I got here. "But it was such a
simple idea!", I'd mutter to myself. "Next time, I'll do it right!" Well, Óli of the past, I think
this may be one of those rare cases when that promise is kept. This website is powered exclusively
by off-the-shelf frameworks and services, and what an excellent decision that turned out to be!
[Astro](https://astro.build/) and [Starlight](https://starlight.astro.build/) have enabled me to
turn a collection of Markdown files into a website I'd need a year to make 😆

### `.com` domains are surprisingly affordable

It's surprising, I know! Maybe you were already aware of this, and you think it's silly of me to
even mention this. Nevertheless, this domain[^1] is the first one I've ever bought, and I'm excited
about it. It was a much cheaper and simpler process than I had initially expected. I looked at a few
domain providers, and the ones I ended up choosing between were
[Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/domains/) and [Porkbun](https://porkbun.com/). Both seemed
to provide excellent value for a great price, so it was a tough call. On a whim, though, I decided
to go with Porkbun. Now, I don't have anything smart to say about _why_ I made that choice; it was
literally down to a coin toss. My experience has been good so far, and I don't doubt I would've had
a nice experience with Cloudflare as well.
